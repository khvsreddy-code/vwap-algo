# v9.4.36 — One-month chart history + historical CE/PE execution labels

- Live NIFTY chart history defaults to 31 calendar days instead of a short rolling window.
- The live NIFTY chart sends the full loaded history to Lightweight Charts instead of truncating to 90 candles.
- Historical BUY CE / BUY PE markers are generated by replaying the same VWAP confirmation state machine used by live/replay.
- Historical markers are attached to the confirmation candle, not the VWAP crossing candle.
- The currently-forming live candle is excluded from historical-signal generation, preventing false historical labels.
- Live execution markers remain sourced from the persistent local execution ledger and override duplicate historical markers on the same candle/type.
- History client and engine seed history defaults were raised to 31 days.
- Replay and standalone historical-chart defaults are also 31 days.
- Existing IST timezone and configurable algo session behavior are preserved.
